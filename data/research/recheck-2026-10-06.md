Scope requested by Nico before public launch:

1. Resolve Brandwatch, Flare, Planet sanctions screens marked `REVIEW REQUIRED`.
2. Verify every material claim in PimEyes and Group-IB YAML profiles against primary / highly credible sources; use neutral wording; include vendor responses.

Lists used (local dumps downloaded/used 2026-10-06):

- US OFAC SDN + consolidated non-SDN (`sdn.csv`, `cons_prim.csv`)

Method note: earlier automated screening used naive substring matching, which produced false positives. Recheck used **whole-word / token-boundary** matching plus manual inspection of each hit.

## 1) Brandwatch — sanctions

**Prior result:** `REVIEW REQUIRED` because screened parent name **Cision** appeared in OFAC/EU/UK dumps.

**What actually matched:** Substring `Cision` inside unrelated tokens such as **PRECISION** / French **PRÉCISION** (examples: *China National Precision Machinery Import/Export Corporation* on OFAC SDN; EU French aliases for precision-machinery institutes). **Not** Cision Ltd / Brandwatch.

**Owners/parents screened:** Brandwatch; Cision / Cision Ltd / Cision US Inc; Platinum Equity (Cision taken private Jan 2020; Brandwatch acquired by Cision Jun 2021).

**Whole-word recheck (OFAC/EU/UK/UN):** No designation of Brandwatch, Cision (as the PR software company), or Platinum Equity.

**Final verdict:** **False positive → CLEAR.** YAML updated.

Sources: OFAC/EU/UK/UN dumps; [Cision closes Brandwatch acquisition](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/cision-closes-brandwatch-acquisition-883471423.html); [Platinum Equity completes Cision acquisition](https://www.platinumequity.com/news/platinum-equity-completes-2-7-billion-acquisition-of-cision/).
